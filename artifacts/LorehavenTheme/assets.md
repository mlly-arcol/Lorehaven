# Lorehaven: 星灯秘阁 / Starlit Archive

## Theme set

- `starlit`: 星灯秘阁 / Starlit Archive — violet ink, warm ivory, candlelit gold, a peaceful home for stories.
- `harbor`: 潮汐手札 / Harbor Journal — the preserved warm-paper, moss-green harbor theme.
- Each style supports light, dark, and system appearance. Existing saved settings retain Harbor Journal; new installations use Starlit Archive.

## Artwork

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Originals remain in the generated-images library; the application uses the copies below. No image-editing script was used.

## Final generation prompts

### Library panorama

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a wide decorative header illustration for Lorehaven desktop storytelling application's Starlit Archive theme, not a UI mockup. Create a polished painterly fantasy illustration, 1792 by 640 landscape. A peaceful ancient library sanctuary at night: tall elegant arched window opening onto a hazy violet starry sky, floor-to-ceiling walnut bookshelves, softly glowing brass reading lamps, a small wooden reading desk and open manuscript. Warm candle gold, muted mauve and dusty indigo, refined textured gouache and fine engraved detail. Calm magical refuge for stories, sophisticated and quiet, not a game battle or cartoon. Composition: architectural scene and warm light mostly in the right two thirds, left third airy and low-contrast with subtle paper-like atmosphere, to sit behind an application heading with a gradient mask. No people, no readable writing, no typography, no logos, no border, no UI. Avoid saturated neon, bright blue, harsh black, excessive sparkles or clutter.

### Book-and-lantern vignette

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent decorative corner vignette for Lorehaven desktop storytelling application's Starlit Archive theme. Create a sophisticated painterly gouache illustration with fine engraved details, square 1024 by 1024 canvas, genuinely transparent background. A small arrangement of an open aged storybook, two closed indigo clothbound books, a warmly glowing antique brass lantern with a tiny star-shaped finial, and a delicate sprig of pale lavender. Warm candle gold and desaturated indigo, restrained magical scholarly mood. Arrange as an elegant low horizontal cluster near the lower half of the canvas, leaving generous transparent space above and around, soft translucent shadow only immediately under objects. Isolated objects, crisp tasteful details readable at small size, no room or scenery, no typography or readable writing, no watermark, no border, no UI, no opaque background, no checkerboard painted into the image.
